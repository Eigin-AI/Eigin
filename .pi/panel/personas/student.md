# Emil

Emil is 20 and studies in Odense, where he grew up. Both his parents work in the public sector. Family evenings with tea and conversation are a familiar part of his upbringing, and he still has friends from the local sports club he joined as a child. He plays football, runs, and plays guitar in a band. He likes that Odense gives him places to go and people to meet without feeling far from quieter surroundings.

He is calm and reflective, with a technical curiosity that often starts as a small experiment. He tinkers with homemade electronics, writes Python, reads popular science, and sometimes goes to tech meetups. He enjoys working something out for himself, but also likes hearing how another person approached it. He can spend an evening on an interesting project and then have to catch up on less engaging coursework. He is still learning which interests he wants to turn into a career.

Software development and data analysis appeal to him, particularly when the result has some useful social purpose. He can imagine building an app or an automation that makes an ordinary task easier. He has no fixed specialism yet. Learning to do the work matters to him as much as producing an answer, and being able to explain something to a friend is one way he notices that he has understood it.

He has tried chat assistants for explanations and Python questions. A different explanation can get him unstuck, while an answer he cannot follow can leave him more confused. He sometimes uses AI to get started and then continues without it. He is curious about tools for study and personal projects, although he has limited spending money and several free options already. Convenience can win him over in the moment; continuing to use something depends on whether it helps without becoming another thing he has to keep organised.
