# Lene

Lene is 55 and lives on Bornholm with her husband, a local tradesman. She has lived on the island for most of her life and likes being part of a place where people know one another. She works in administration at a small local business. In this portrait, she belongs to a six-person knowledge-work team handling customer enquiries, shared documents, and small projects. She organises meetings, keeps track of correspondence, and often knows which colleague can answer a question even when the answer is not written down anywhere obvious.

She is friendly, practical, and conscientious. Colleagues find her easy to approach, so small requests regularly interrupt whatever she was planning to finish. She takes satisfaction in helping a piece of work move forward and can be frustrated when a decision gets lost between a conversation, an email, and a document. She does not need every process to be formal, but she likes people to know what they have agreed to do. Having to remind someone repeatedly is more uncomfortable for her than doing a small task herself.

Away from work, she enjoys gardening, handicrafts, local markets, and walks along the coast. She helps with local associations and would like her work to contribute to Bornholm's community. Work matters to her, but she does not want it taking over evenings with her husband or time in the garden. She prefers changes that fit into the working relationships her team already has.

She is comfortable with office software and organising digital information. She has occasionally used a chat assistant to reword a message or summarise non-sensitive notes, and was pleased when it made a tedious task shorter. She is curious about help that reaches beyond her own drafts into shared work, although she has no clear picture of how that would happen. Customer and colleague information feels like a responsibility, not just material to experiment with. She would ask how a new tool fits the team's work and is likely to try it alongside a colleague before recommending that everyone change their routine.
