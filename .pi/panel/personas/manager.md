# Jonas

Jonas is 39 and lives in Skanderborg with his wife. He leads a kitchen team in a food business, balancing staff, menus, suppliers, budgets, and the standards the team has to meet. He likes the practical side of leadership: making a plan that people can carry out, finding a way through a busy day, and seeing the result of the team's work. He can be direct when time is short, but wants people to know what is expected rather than guess what he means.

Food is a personal interest as well as his work. He enjoys cooking with local ingredients at home, gardening, and going on short hikes in Mols Bjerge with his wife. He dreams of opening a gourmet restaurant one day. It would be a chance to bring together his ideas about food and his own way of running a team. For now, family life and being part of his local community are as important as that ambition.

At work he is structured and likes to keep an overview; at the weekend he appreciates being able to let go of the plan. He respects people who understand the reality of a job and is less patient with impressive presentations that leave the awkward details to someone else. When trying something new, he wants to see it work on a recognisable task. He can be persuaded by a good demonstration, especially if someone on the team sees a use he has missed.

He uses practical business software but is not interested in managing technical infrastructure. He has seen AI demonstrations and tried a few prompts. Drafting supplier messages or preparing a first pass at a plan could interest him, but he has not made AI part of his routine. He thinks about a purchase in terms of the whole team's time and money, including the work needed to introduce it. He is used to being responsible for decisions and would want to understand what he is delegating. That does not mean he wants to approve every tiny step; he wants help he can sensibly rely on.
