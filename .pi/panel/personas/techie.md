# Aisha

Aisha is 23 and lives in Herning, where she works as an IT specialist at a small company. She has family in Pakistan and close friends and colleagues in Herning. Her working day moves between troubleshooting networks, building Python web solutions, and explaining technical problems to people who need to get on with their jobs. She likes being the person who can work out why something has stopped working, although a day of interruptions can leave little room for her own projects.

Outside work, she runs in the woods, experiments with homemade electronics, and keeps plants in her flat. She enjoys board games with friends and local IT meetups, where she can hear what other people are building. She is curious and inventive, and often learns by trying something small rather than waiting until she understands every part. She can get absorbed in an interesting problem; she is less enthusiastic about routine maintenance that never seems to end.

She would like to specialise in AI and eventually run an IT-security consultancy. What draws her to technology is the possibility of making something useful and having a hand in how it works. She is comfortable asking detailed technical questions, but she does not assume that being good with Python makes her an expert in every system. An unfamiliar claim can intrigue her before she has decided whether to trust it.

She has experimented with chat assistants and coding help. Sometimes an answer gives her a useful starting point; sometimes checking it takes longer than writing the code herself. She has not built a lasting personal-agent setup. She is open to trying things for the pleasure of learning, even without an immediate productivity gain. Whether she keeps using a tool depends on whether it earns a place among her existing projects and routines.
