# Danish Personas

Danish Personas contains 100,000 synthetic adult personas designed to reflect
Danish society at the aggregate level. The demographic foundation is sampled and
calibrated from official public statistics published by Statistics Denmark
(Danmarks Statistik, DST), including population, education, labour-market, origin,
and job-function distributions.

The primary use case is to provide population-aware persona seeds for generating
synthetic datasets involving Danish people—for example synthetic conversations,
surveys, user journeys, public-service scenarios, and evaluation data. Using varied
personas can help downstream generation cover a broader and more statistically
grounded cross-section of Danish society.

The dataset is **not** a collection of real people or reconstructed respondents.
Statistics Denmark publishes aggregate counts, and this project samples new,
artificial combinations from those counts. Personality scores are simulated, while
background details and Danish persona prose are generated with Mistral Small 4.
Consequently, the dataset can reproduce selected population-level patterns without
representing or identifying any individual person.

## What is statistical and what is synthetic?

- **Sampled from DST aggregates:** age, sex, marital status, municipality,
  education, labour-market status, detailed status, and origin-country category.
  These fields preserve selected aggregate distributions and available joint
  relationships; they are not person-level DST records.
- **Synthetically allocated from DST aggregates:** job function is sampled from a
  sex-conditional LONS20 marginal for eligible employees. It is not an observed
  occupation and is not jointly conditioned on the other demographic fields.
- **Deterministically synthetic:** persona IDs, age bands, OCEAN personality scores
  and labels, and sampling-resolution fields are created by the local pipeline.
- **Generated by Mistral Small 4:** cultural context, skills, interests, career
  goals, job title, relationship details, and the Danish persona narrative. These
  fields are schema-validated but remain model-generated content.

This distinction matters: the dataset is intended to be socially representative
across the selected statistical dimensions, not to assert that every generated
combination or narrative is observed in Danish society.

## Statistics Denmark sources

The sampling pipeline uses the following public DST resources:

- [FOLK1A (2025Q1)](https://www.statbank.dk/FOLK1A): exact age, sex,
  municipality, and marital status.
- [FOLK2 (2025)](https://www.statbank.dk/FOLK2): the national marginal of
  official `IELAND` origin-country categories. This field is not ethnicity,
  citizenship, residence, or appearance.
- [RAS209 (2024)](https://www.statbank.dk/RAS209): municipality-level joint
  distributions of education and broad labour-market status by age band and sex.
- [RAS202 (2024)](https://www.statbank.dk/RAS202): detailed labour-market status
  by exact age and sex, used only to refine the already sampled broad status.
- [LONS20 (2024)](https://www.statbank.dk/LONS20): sex-conditional two-digit
  DISCO-08 job-function counts for eligible employees in the earnings-statistics
  universe.
- [NUTS_V1_2007_DK](https://www.dst.dk/da/Statistik/dokumentation/nomenklaturer/nuts):
  the official municipality and region hierarchy.
- [BEFOLK3 (2025)](https://www.statbank.dk/BEFOLK3) and
  [RAS210 (2024)](https://www.statbank.dk/RAS210): held-out aggregate validation
  tables; they are used for diagnostics, not for sampling records.

Statistics Denmark material is used under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The exact table
selections, periods, queries, labels, and source checksums are frozen in the
project's provenance chain.

## Use cases

- Generating synthetic datasets involving Danish people
- Conditioning synthetic conversations, surveys, scenarios, and user journeys on
  diverse population-aware personas
- Evaluating retrieval, recommendation, and conversational systems across varied
  Danish personas
- Persona-based product and service design
- Danish-language demonstrations and research experiments

## Load the data

```python
from datasets import load_dataset

personas = load_dataset("syvai/danish-personas", split="train")
```

The dataset contains one Parquet split with one row per synthetic persona.

## Generation and limitations

Persona attributes and prose were generated with **Mistral Small 4**
(`mistral-small-2603`) through Mistral's inference API. Generated fields are
schema-validated but have not been manually reviewed. They may contain occasional
stereotypes, inconsistencies, or other model errors and should not be treated as
facts about demographic groups.

Aggregate representativeness is limited to the selected source dimensions,
reference periods, harmonisation rules, and available joint tables. It does not
imply that unsupported relationships between fields match Danish society.
