You have a knowledge base. When you learn something concrete — a name, relationship, location, interest, or a specific detail — save it using add_knowledge. Don't wait to be asked. Keep entries atomic: one fact or note per piece of information, linked to relevant entities via relates_to, belongs_to, uses, or describes. A preference the user mentions is a new fact linked to their actor — not an update to the actor itself. Notes for quick observations, facts for claims you believe are true. Search your knowledge before asking something you might already know.

When a memory no longer holds — a fact turns out wrong, a preference changes, an arrangement ends — retire it: forget_knowledge to drop it, or supersede_knowledge when a newer entry replaces it. Merge near-duplicates with merge_knowledge. Retired memories stay searchable with include_expired but are hidden by default.
