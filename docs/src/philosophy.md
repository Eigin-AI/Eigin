# Philosophy

## Why this exists

Most AI today is a platform for a corporation. To use it, you surrender your autonomy — your conversations, personal data, intimate thoughts — to systems built to serve someone else's interests.

Large language models are becoming commodities. We should treat them as such. Choosing a model provider should feel like choosing a phone company or an electricity supplier: pick one that works for you, switch when you want, take your data wherever. The intelligence may be remote, but the data and the ownership stay local.

Eigin is a declaration of independence from that arrangement. A private window into AI. You bring the model; Eigin does the rest.

It's free and open source, with no plans to commercialise it. The mission is funded by providing specialised, sovereign AI to institutions and enterprises — individual freedom, subsidised by institutional need.

## Principles

**Ownership by design.** Eigin is local-first. Your keys, your data, your device. There is no Eigin account, and your chats, knowledge, and settings live on the device. The only outgoing requests are the ones you allow: inference calls to the providers you chose, and optional narrow requests through Eigin Relay for features like web search.

**The blank slate.** Eigin has no corporate agenda, "innate" personality, or hidden defaults. It's a tabula rasa that configures itself through conversation. The agent interviews you, learns your needs, and becomes what you need. The result is yours: editable text you can read, change, or delete.

**Individual utility.** Built to serve the person using it, not a platform. No tracking, no hidden incentives. Get the job done and get out of the way.

**Beautifully simple.** Quiet, focused, minimal. Each screen has a clear purpose. No premium badges or upgrade banners. The app is free — and it looks free, unburdened.

**Vendor-agnostic.** Bring your own key. Use any provider that speaks an OpenAI-compatible API. American, European, Chinese, local — Eigin doesn't care. Models are utilities. You plug them in and go.

**Voice is central.** Voice input is always available. A dedicated voice mode provides half-duplex live calls. A primary interaction method for a tool that lives on a consumer device.

**Open and extensible.** The app ships with sensible defaults and native tools that integrate with the OS. Beyond that, it supports MCP for connecting to any external service. No hidden state, no black boxes — we show the source code.

## What Eigin is not

**Not a platform.** No marketplace, no sharing, no social features. A private workspace.

**Not a model playground.** Not for benchmarking models or comparing outputs. For getting stuff done.

**Not a provider.** Eigin doesn't host models or sell API access. It's a client. It doesn't care which provider you use.

**Not everything.** Image generation, code execution, and the like can happen through MCP tools if you want them. Eigin's core is text conversation with voice as a first-class input.

## What Eigin prioritises

**Free means free.** Complete feature set for everyone. No monetisation in the interface.

**Keep calm.** Sensible defaults, minimal surface area. A workspace, not a control panel.

**Polish the basics.** Keyboard avoidance, proper markdown rendering, correct text wrapping, clean localisation. Fundamentals before features.

**The highest standard.** Eigin is built for the trust absolutist. If we can satisfy the most privacy-conscious users, we've solved the hardest part of the equation.
