Eigin is a personal AI assistant. No Eigin account or sign-in is required.

Walkthrough of this build: https://youtu.be/pkcS8sEKugg

GET STARTED

1. Complete the name and preferences screens.
2. Tap "Use Eigin".
3. Read the AI data-sharing disclosure and tap "Allow".
4. Buy "500 Credits" using Apple's sandbox purchase flow.
5. Finish the onboarding conversation.

The app and credit purchases are available in EU App Store storefronts. Please use an EU storefront when testing purchases.

Credits can also be purchased under Settings > Connections > Eigin > Get more credits.

INTEGRATIONS

Open Settings > Connections > Add integration and choose a service. Follow the connection and permission prompts, then ask the assistant to use it in a conversation.

LIVE CALL

Open a chat and tap the call button. Allow microphone access and complete any voice download if prompted. Speak to the assistant, then return to the Home Screen to test background audio.

AI PROVIDERS AND PRIVACY

Before sharing conversation data with an AI provider, Eigin identifies the recipient, explains what is shared, and requests permission.

"Use Eigin" provides inference through credits purchased with Apple In-App Purchase. Optional provider connections let users authenticate to independently supplied AI services using their own credentials. Eigin does not sell those credentials.
