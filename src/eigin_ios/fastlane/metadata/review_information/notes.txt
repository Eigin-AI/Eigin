Eigin is a personal AI assistant. There is no account and no sign-in. Our self-assessment against the App Store Review Guidelines is at https://docs.eigin.ai/compliance/app-store.html

Eigin is conversational. Not all features have a dedicated screen. Some you simply ask for. The empty chat shows suggestion cards for the main areas.

A short walkthrough video: https://youtu.be/21q-JgungI8

GETTING STARTED

1. Open the app. Onboarding asks for your name and what you want help with.
2. On the connect step, tap "Use Eigin", then Buy on "500 Credits" (consumable in-app purchase). Sandbox purchases grant credits right away.
3. Onboarding ends with a short conversation. Ask the assistant to wrap it up whenever you like.

Onboarding always uses cloud AI, so either credits or your own provider is required to finish it. An on-device model is available afterwards under Settings > Connections; it needs an iPhone 15 or newer.

THINGS TO TRY

- Live call: tap the call button in the chat toolbar. A hands-free voice conversation on the native iOS call screen. Put the phone away and it keeps going on headphones, backgrounded or with the screen locked.
- Apple Health: use the "Health" suggestion group, or say "Log a banana and 200 g of skyr for breakfast", then "What have I eaten today?". Log something first. If there is no data in Apple Health, a read returns nothing.
- Calendar, reminders, web search, and memory: just ask. Eigin confirms before it creates or changes anything.

CREDITS

Credits are consumable in-app purchases. "Recover credits" re-applies purchases made on this Apple Account, so a balance survives a reinstall.

PERMISSIONS

Calendar, reminders, location, health, microphone, speech recognition, camera, photos, and local network (for email) are all optional, and requested only when the related feature is first used.

If anything blocks you, email us at support@eigin.ai.
