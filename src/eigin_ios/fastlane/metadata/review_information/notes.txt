Eigin is a personal AI assistant. There is no account and no sign-in. Our self-assessment against the App Store Review Guidelines is at https://docs.eigin.ai/compliance/app-store.html

GETTING STARTED

1. Open the app. Onboarding asks for your name and what you want help with.
2. On the connect step, tap "Use Eigin". A credits sheet opens.
3. Tap Buy on "500 Credits" (consumable in-app purchase). Sandbox purchases are supported by our server, so the purchase grants credits right away and you will see "You're good to go".
4. Onboarding ends with a short conversation. You can ask the assistant to wrap it up whenever you like.

After that you are in a normal chat. You can ask about the app, tell it things to remember, ask it to search the web, or ask it to add a calendar event or reminder. It asks for confirmation before it creates or changes anything.

Onboarding always uses cloud AI, so either an Eigin credits purchase or your own provider (via "Connect your own provider") is required to complete it. The app discloses that cloud requests are processed by a third-party AI provider. An on-device model is available afterwards under Settings > Connections; it needs an iPhone 15 or newer.

PERMISSIONS

Calendar, reminders, location, health, microphone, speech recognition, camera, photos, and local network (for email) are all optional and requested only when the related feature is first used.

If anything blocks you, email us at support@eigin.ai.
