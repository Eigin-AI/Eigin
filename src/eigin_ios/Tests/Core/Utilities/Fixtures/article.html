Rivers burst their banks as record rain hits the coast

By Mara Jensen2 hours ago

Emergency crews were deployed overnight as flooding reached the harbour district. Residents of the old town were moved to three temporary shelters, city officials said.

The heaviest rainfall in forty years measured 184 mm in 24 hours, according to the national weather service. Roads remain closed and power is out for roughly 12,000 households.

What happens next

Forecasters expect the rain to ease by Thursday. A full damage assessment is expected on Friday, though officials warned early estimates were very uncertain.
