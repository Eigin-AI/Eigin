# Participation terms

## 1. Who we are

**Eigin ApS**, CVR 46428765, Denmark. Questions: [contribute@eigin.ai](mailto:contribute@eigin.ai).

## 2. Taking part

- You must be **18 or older**, contributing in your own name, and have the right to submit your work.
- You must agree to the [Eigin Individual CLA](https://docs.eigin.ai/contributing/individual-agreement.html) and these terms.
- The call sets the topic, deadline, selection criteria, and funding amounts. Submission alone does not guarantee payment.

- Leave out confidential material and personal information from your submission that you do not have permission to share.

## 3. Selection and credit

We choose **one selected spec and one runner-up** using the criteria in the call. We contact their authors and publish both specs with our reasons for choosing them.

By submitting, you agree to this publication:

- **Your name** appears with your spec.
- **Your Git author email** appears in public commit history. Your separate contact email stays private.
- **Copies of your spec** may remain in mirrors and Git history, even if we remove it from our own site or repository.

Selection does not promise implementation or further paid work.

## 4. Payment

- The selected and runner-up authors receive the amounts in the call. Other submissions are unpaid.
- Payment depends on us being legally able to pay you from Denmark, including meeting applicable sanctions and accounting requirements.
- Where those requirements are met, we pay within **10 working days** of receiving the required payment details and documentation after selection.
- You handle your own tax reporting.

## 5. Withdrawal

Email [contribute@eigin.ai](mailto:contribute@eigin.ai) to withdraw before selection. We will stop considering your spec for funding and publication through the call. Rights already granted under the CLA remain in place.

## 6. Privacy

We use your GitLab identity, submission, contact details, and acceptance records to run the call and document agreements. Payment details are used for payment and accounting. Unselected specs are not published.

Email [contribute@eigin.ai](mailto:contribute@eigin.ai) to ask about access, correction, or deletion. Public attribution and required legal records may need to remain.
