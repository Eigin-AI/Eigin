Coding agents make it faster to write code. But good engineering takes judgement: understanding a problem, weighing trade-offs, and setting the right direction.

Our experiment is to fund that thinking. Share a spec for one of our open problems, on your own or with a coding agent. We fund selected specs, publish them with credit to you, and take on the implementation.

Here's how it works.

<!-- foldouts -->

## 1. Find an open call

Each request for spec describes a problem we're working on, what we can pay for your help, the deadline, and what we'll focus on when reviewing submissions.

Choose a problem that interests you, explore the context, and share your approach. The [spec guide](/guides/spec) explains what makes a useful submission.

## 2. Submit your spec

Sign in with your account from [code.eigin.ai](https://code.eigin.ai) and upload your spec as Markdown. You can replace it until the deadline.

Only you and the Eigin team can read your submission. Other contributors cannot see it, and unselected specs remain private after the call closes.

## 3. We review and select

We'll review the submissions for how well they address the problem. We select one spec as the basis for implementation and one runner-up as a valuable contribution to our thinking.

Those two contributions receive the amounts published in the call. We'll contact participants with the outcome. The [participation terms](/guides/participation-terms) explain who can take part and how funding works.

## 4. Publish with credit

We publish both specs on this site with their authors' names and a note on what we like about them. Everyone can read the work and learn from it.

We'll commit the specs to our repo with you as the Git author. We handle the merge request, so there's nothing more you need to do. But you're always welcome to [contribute to other issues](https://code.eigin.ai/dev/eigin/-/issues).

Our repo mirrors to [GitHub](https://github.com/Eigin-AI/Eigin). To link your contribution to your GitHub profile, make sure the Git author email you use here is also associated with your GitHub account.

## 5. Arrange payment

We contact the selected contributor and runner-up to arrange payment. Payment timing and requirements are set out in the [participation terms](/guides/participation-terms).

<!-- end-foldouts -->

## Help us shape the format

We believe that thinking carefully about a problem is valuable work. We want to give people a way to contribute that work, receive credit, and be rewarded for it.

This is new. We're figuring out whether it works and feels fair. We welcome any feedback you have. You can always [chat with us on Matrix](https://chat.eigin.ai).
